# Pitch script: the project, and the "it didn't sound like me" story

Presenter: Rand. About 2½ minutes. *(Italics = what to do, not what to say.)*

---

## 1. The hook (15s)

*(Face the judges.)*

> Everyone has a decision they keep putting off. Should I quit? Should I move? Should I do the master's?
> We built a courtroom where you put that decision on trial, and the only witnesses are **you, from the future.**

## 2. What it does (40s)

*(Start the demo on the home screen.)*

> You read one short paragraph out loud. From that, ElevenLabs **clones your voice**, and we pull out who you are: your age, your job, how much risk you like.
> You take a selfie, and your face goes onto every character.
> Then you ask your question.

*(Ask the question, or click a suggestion.)*

> The Judge sets the agenda. Then two versions of you argue it out, **in your own voice**:
> the best future you, at the moment that life is going best, and the worst future you, at the moment it's gone wrong.
> If one of them says something you disagree with, you hold Space and object, and they have to answer you.
> At the end, the Judge doesn't just say yes or no. They show you **where you land**, from "strong no" to "strong yes", and why.

## 3. The voice story (60s), the part judges remember

*(Slow down here. This is the human bit.)*

> The whole idea only works if it sounds like *you*. And the first time I cloned my own voice… I came out **American**.
> I'm British. My own future self had a different accent from me.
>
> So I tried the obvious fix: I told ElevenLabs to use a British accent. And then I sounded *too* British. Like a costume.
>
> So we stopped guessing and tested it properly. We made **more than twenty versions** of my voice, and we checked every one by machine,
> transcribing them with ElevenLabs' own speech-to-text, so we knew exactly what each setting did.
> We learned three things:
> - the fast models drifted American, and the full **Eleven v4** model held on to my accent best;
> - the recording matters more than any setting, so we cut the cleanest two minutes out of a twenty-minute recording;
> - and a *slight* accent hint, before every sentence, works where a strong one turns into a caricature.
>
> And here's the important bit for you: **when *you* use it, we don't add any accent at all.** Your clone keeps your own voice.
> We record you raw, with no noise filter, because filters strip out exactly the detail that makes an accent. And if ElevenLabs can't use your clone, the app tells you, instead of quietly swapping in a stranger's voice.

*(Optional, if it's getting laughs:)*

> We even tried ElevenLabs' professional voice cloning. It needs thirty minutes of audio and a voice check…
> which locked me out for twenty-one hours. So instant cloning it is.

## 4. Close (20s)

> Five ElevenLabs products in one courtroom: voice cloning, Eleven v4 voices with emotion, Scribe speech-to-text, and sound effects and music we generated.
> And when it's over, you can save your trial and take it home on your phone. It's deleted after seven days.
>
> Your future is on trial. Want to take the stand?

*(Hand a judge the laptop.)*

---

## If a judge asks

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Is that really my voice?" | Yes: an instant clone from the paragraph you just read, made in about ten seconds |
| "Why did it sound American at first?" | The faster models drift towards an American accent. The full Eleven v4 model, at maximum similarity, with a clean recording, keeps it |
| "Do you change my accent?" | No. The accent hint is only on the presenter's demo voice. Yours is untouched |
| "What do you keep?" | Nothing, unless you choose to. Saving your trial keeps the words for 7 days. Your voice clone is deleted after the demo |
